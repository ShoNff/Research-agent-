# Anguilla Summer 2026 — June 26–30 Trip Guide

A practical, itinerary-friendly guide to four days on Anguilla — **Friday June 26 through Tuesday June 30, 2026** — for travelers based at **Zemi Beach House** on Shoal Bay East, with a rental car. Built around the island's live music, food, beaches, and attractions, and timed to the days of the week so you hit each venue when it's actually open.

![Anguilla from above — the low, reef-fringed island in the northern Leeward Islands (Wikimedia Commons)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Anguilla-aerial%20view%20western%20portion.jpg?width=1280)

## Executive Summary

Anguilla is a flat, low-traffic British Caribbean island whose reputation rests on three things: **world-class beaches**, an outsized **restaurant and beach-bar scene**, and **live music** — much of it built around one man, Bankie Banx, and his Dune Preserve. You're staying at [Zemi Beach House](https://www.zemibeach.com/) on the island's quieter **East End**, on the famous Shoal Bay East, with five restaurants and a 300-year-old Thai spa house on property. With a car, the whole island is 20–30 minutes away.

The single most important planning fact for these dates: **the Dune Preserve is closed on Mondays**, and several signature experiences (Scilly Cay, Elvis' DJ nights, Sunday beach barbecues) are **day-of-week specific**. Your four nights — Fri / Sat / Sun / Mon — line up almost perfectly with the island's rhythm if you sequence them right. The plan below does that.

### Your dates, by day of week
- **Friday, June 26** — arrival; Dune Preserve Friday BBQ + live music
- **Saturday, June 27** — boat/snorkel day; Elvis' Beach Bar DJ night
- **Sunday, June 28** — Scilly Cay lunch and/or the legendary Sunday "Dune day"
- **Monday, June 29** — east-end culture, beaches, Zemi spa, fine dining (Dune is closed)
- **Tuesday, June 30** — departure morning

## Your Base: Zemi Beach House (Shoal Bay East)

[Zemi Beach House](https://www.zemibeach.com/), an LXR/Hilton resort, sits on six oceanfront acres on Anguilla's **East End along Shoal Bay East** — consistently rated among the best beaches in the Caribbean and the world. On property you have five places to eat and drink, so a low-key first or last night never requires leaving:

- **Stone** — Asian-inspired menus with ocean views (fine dining)
- **20 Knots** — Caribbean and Latin American cuisine, beachfront
- **Rhum Room** — a rum-and-cigar bar set inside a **300-year-old Thai house**, with rare rums to sample
- **Zemi Beach Club** — casual all-day beach bar and inventive cocktails
- **Zemi Thai House Spa** — Turkish hammam, outdoor soaking pool, full treatment menu (worth booking one slow afternoon)

Because you're on the East End, the nearby attractions (Heritage Collection Museum, Big Spring petroglyphs, Island Harbour) are the closest things to "walk-out-the-door" sightseeing — useful for the Monday when the island's nightlife is quietest.

## Getting Around: Driving in Anguilla

Anguilla drives on the **left** (it's a British territory), but the island is flat, the speed limit is **30 mph** (20 in villages and school zones), and there's very little traffic — most visitors adjust within a day. The right-hand turns are the only real adjustment.

- **You'll need a temporary Anguilla driving permit** — your rental company arranges it on the spot. Bring your home license and a credit card; the one-time fee is about **US$25** and it's valid for months.
- **Main roads are paved**; many scenic cut-throughs and beach access tracks are sandy or rutted. A higher-clearance vehicle helps if you want to chase the back-road beaches, and take unpaved sections slowly at night.
- A car is the right call here: it unlocks the far-flung beach bars and restaurants (Rendezvous Bay, Sandy Ground, Meads Bay) that make Anguilla, Anguilla.

## Live Music & Nightlife

Anguilla's music scene is intimate — small venues, toes in the sand, the band an arm's length away — and it runs on a weekly schedule. Here's what's on during your stay.

### Dune Preserve (Bankie Banx) — Rendezvous Bay
The island's iconic, hand-built driftwood beach bar, owned by **Bankie Banx**, one of Anguilla's most beloved musicians, and home to the March Moonsplash festival. It's the single best music venue on the island.
- **Open daily except Monday**, noon to sunset for drinks and light fare
- **Nightly live entertainment Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday**
- **Friday BBQ 7:30–9:30pm**
- **Sunday is the big one** — live music roughly **1:00–6:30pm**, a full island-wide scene
- **Closed Monday** — plan accordingly (see the Monday itinerary)

### Elvis' Beach Bar — Sandy Ground
A lively hangout built out of a repurposed boat hull, right on the sand at Sandy Ground, with a giant outdoor screen and a true local-meets-traveler crowd.
- **DJ Kue — one of Anguilla's hottest DJs — plays Saturday nights**
- Hosts one of the island's favorite **Sunday beach barbecues**

### Also worth knowing
- **Da'Vida** (Crocus Bay) runs a popular **Sunday beach barbecue** as well.
- Local artists to watch for on any night's listings: **Bankie Banx, Omari Banks, the Musical Brothers** — reggae, soca, and smooth jazz.
- Check the live calendar close to your trip; weekly entertainment listings are kept current at [anguilla-beaches.com/anguilla-events](https://www.anguilla-beaches.com/anguilla-events.html) and on each venue's own site ([Dune Preserve](https://www.bankiebanx.net/dunepreserve), [Elvis' Beach Bar](https://www.elvisbeachbar.com/entertainment-nightlife)).

## Must-See Beaches

![Rendezvous Bay, Anguilla — long, calm, and walkable to the island's best beach bars and music (Wikimedia Commons)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Cuisinart,%20Rendezvous%20Bay,%20Anguilla%202009.jpg?width=1280)

Anguilla has 33 beaches; these are the ones to prioritize in four days.

- **Shoal Bay East** — *at your doorstep.* Anguilla's most famous beach: bright, shallow, almost seaweed-free water clear enough to see the bottom 50 yards out. Easy swimming and long shoreline walks straight from Zemi.
- **Rendezvous Bay** — long, open, and calm, with views across to St. Martin/St. Maarten and walking access to the island's best beach bars and music (the Dune is here). Family-friendly.
- **Meads Bay** — over two miles of soft white sand on the northwest coast; home to Blanchards and several top restaurants.
- **Little Bay** — a tiny, dramatic cove framed by layered limestone cliffs; reached by boat or by rope down the rocks. Some of the island's best easy snorkeling.
- **Sandy Island** — a postcard islet a short boat hop off Sandy Ground; shallow turquoise water, a single beach-grill bar, classic castaway feel.
- **Shoal Bay West** — quieter than its eastern namesake; home to the sleek newer **Savi Beach Club**.

## Food: From Beach Shacks to Fine Dining

Anguilla punches far above its size on food. A rough map:

### Fine dining
- **Blanchards** (Meads Bay) — an island legend; refined-but-relaxed global twist on Caribbean cuisine, steps from the sand.
- **Veya** (Sandy Ground) — Chef Carrie Bogar's "cuisine of the sun"; the five-course **tasting menu (~$95)** is a highlight.
- **Hibernia** (Island Harbour, East End) — eccentric Franco-Asian cooking and an art gallery; close to Zemi.
- **Stone** at Zemi — your on-property fine-dining option.

### Beach bars & casual local favorites
- **Tasty's** (South Hill) — a perennial pick for authentic Anguillan cooking.
- **Smokey's at the Cove** (Cove Bay) — beachfront local classics; ribs, johnnycakes, crayfish.
- **Elvis' Beach Bar** (Sandy Ground) — no-frills, flavor-packed bites, cold drinks, reggae.
- **Scilly Cay** (off Island Harbour) — see below; fresh grilled lobster and crayfish with famous rum punch.
- **Dolce Vita** (Sandy Ground) — award-winning Italian beach restaurant; homemade pasta, sand-front tables.
- **Savi Beach Club** (Shoal Bay West) — modern, lounge-y, newer addition to the scene.

## Attractions & Activities

### On the water (the island's signature day out)
- **Sandy Island** and **Prickly Pear Cays** — short boat trips to tiny uninhabited cays for snorkeling, beach time, and grilled-lunch beach bars. Operators include **Tradition Sailing Charters**, Gotcha Garfield Sea Tours, and budget glass-bottom options like Junior's and Nature Boy.
- **Little Bay** snorkeling — crystal-clear, sheltered water beneath limestone cliffs.

### Scilly Cay — a Sunday institution

![Scilly Cay, off Island Harbour, Anguilla — the family-owned private island grill (Wikimedia Commons)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Island%20Harbour-Scilly%20Cay-Anguilla.jpg?width=1280)

A tiny family-owned private island off **Island Harbour**, transformed into a one-of-a-kind beachfront grill known for fresh grilled crayfish/lobster, strong rum punch, and live Sunday music. **Open Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays (≈11:00am–3:30pm)** — so **Sunday June 28 is your window.** To get there, drive to the Island Harbour fishing dock and **wave toward the island**; the free ferry comes to collect you (about a 2-minute ride). Island Harbour is ~10 minutes from Zemi.

### Heritage & culture (great for the Monday)
- **Heritage Collection Museum** (East End) — Colville Petty's OBE-recognized personal collection: Arawak tools, plantation-era artifacts, island history. Small building, big payoff. Close to Zemi.
- **Big Spring** (Island Harbour) — a collapsed cave national park with 60+ **Arawak "Spirit Eyes" petroglyphs** carved between AD 600–1200, behind The Pub.
- **Wallblake House** (The Valley) — the island's only surviving 1787 plantation house, with intact kitchen, stable, and slave quarters.
- **Pyrat Rum Distillery** — spirit tasting; horseback riding, biking, and birdwatching (136 species) round out the land options.

## A Suggested 4-Day Itinerary

> Sequenced to the calendar — note the Monday adjustment when the Dune is closed.

### Friday, June 26 — Arrive & ease in
- Land, pick up the car, sort the **temporary driving permit** at the rental desk.
- Check in at Zemi; first swim on **Shoal Bay East** right out front.
- **Evening:** drive to **Rendezvous Bay** for the **Dune Preserve Friday BBQ (7:30–9:30pm)** with live music — the perfect "you've arrived in Anguilla" night. (Prefer something polished? **Blanchards** on Meads Bay.)

### Saturday, June 27 — Boat & beach-bar night
- **Boat day:** half-day charter to **Sandy Island** or **Prickly Pear** for snorkeling and a grilled beach lunch (book Tradition Sailing or similar in advance).
- Late afternoon back at Zemi — pool, **Rhum Room**, or a spa slot.
- **Evening:** **Elvis' Beach Bar** at Sandy Ground for **DJ Kue's Saturday night**; dinner nearby at **Veya** or **Dolce Vita**.

### Sunday, June 28 — The quintessential Anguilla Sunday
- **Lunch at Scilly Cay** — drive to Island Harbour (~10 min), wave for the ferry, eat grilled crayfish with rum punch and live music on a private island.
- **Afternoon/evening:** the famous **Sunday at the Dune** (live music ~1:00–6:30pm) on Rendezvous Bay, or a **Sunday beach BBQ at Da'Vida or Elvis'**. Pick one scene and settle in.

### Monday, June 29 — East-end culture & a slow luxe day *(Dune closed)*
- Morning: **Shoal Bay East** beach day from the resort, or a snorkel at **Little Bay**.
- Midday culture loop near Zemi: **Heritage Collection Museum** → **Big Spring petroglyphs** at Island Harbour.
- Afternoon: **Zemi Thai House spa** treatment.
- **Dinner:** **Hibernia** (East End) or **Stone** on property — both close to home for your last night.

### Tuesday, June 30 — Departure
- Early swim and breakfast at Zemi; one last Shoal Bay walk before the drive to the airport/ferry.

## Planning Notes & Caveats

- **Live-music schedules are weekly patterns, not guarantees.** Specific artist line-ups for these exact 2026 dates aren't published this far out. The day-of-week structure above (Dune Fri/Sat/Sun, Dune closed Mon, Elvis' DJ Sat, Scilly Cay Sun) is the reliable backbone — **reconfirm a week ahead** via each venue's site or the island events calendar.
- **Late June is low/shoulder season** — quieter, warmer, and some restaurants run reduced days or close for a stretch; call ahead to confirm hours and to book the bigger names (Blanchards, Veya, Hibernia) and any boat charter.
- **No major festival falls in these dates** — Moonsplash is in March and the Anguilla Summer Festival/Carnival is late July–early August — so you'll get the island at its relaxed, uncrowded best.
