Day 1 · Friday June 26

Arrive & ease in

☀️ Daytime

  • Land, grab the car, sort the $25 temporary driving permit at the rental desk
  • Check in at Zemi; first swim on Shoal Bay East right out front
  • Sunset drink at the Zemi Beach Club or Rhum Room

🌙 Evening

  • Dune Preserve Friday BBQ (7:30–9:30pm) on Rendezvous Bay — live music, the perfect arrival night
  • Prefer polished? Blanchards on Meads Bay
Day 2 · Saturday June 27

Boat & beach-bar night

⛵ Daytime

  • Half-day charter to Sandy Island or Prickly Pear — snorkel + grilled beach lunch
  • Book Tradition Sailing (or Gotcha / Junior's) ahead
  • Late afternoon: pool, Rhum Room, or a spa slot at Zemi

🌙 Evening

  • Elvis' Beach Bar, Sandy Ground — DJ Kue's Saturday night
  • Dinner nearby: Veya or Dolce Vita
Day 3 · Sunday June 28

The quintessential Anguilla Sunday

🦞 Midday

  • Scilly Cay — drive to Island Harbour (~10 min from Zemi), wave for the free ferry
  • Grilled crayfish + rum punch + live music on a private island (open Sun, ~11–3:30)

🎶 Afternoon

  • Sunday at the Dune — live music ~1:00–6:30pm on Rendezvous Bay
  • Or a Sunday beach BBQ at Da'Vida or Elvis'
  • Pick one scene and settle in
Day 4 · Monday June 29 · Dune closed

East-end culture & a slow luxe day

🏖️ Morning

Shoal Bay East from the resort, or a snorkel at Little Bay.

🏛️ Midday

Heritage Collection Museum → Big Spring petroglyphs at Island Harbour — both minutes from Zemi.

💆 Afternoon

Zemi Thai House spa — hammam + treatment in a 300-yr-old Thai house.

🍽️ Dinner

Hibernia (East End) or Stone on property — close to home for the last night.
Departure Tue June 30: early swim, breakfast, one last Shoal Bay walk before the airport.
Must-see beaches

Six to prioritize

🌟 Shoal Bay East

At your doorstep. Bright, shallow, near seaweed-free, clear 50 yds out.

🎶 Rendezvous Bay

Long & calm, St-Martin views, the island's best beach bars & music.

🍽️ Meads Bay

2+ miles of sand; home to Blanchards & top restaurants.

🤿 Little Bay

Tiny limestone cove, some of the best easy snorkeling.

🏝️ Sandy Island

Postcard islet off Sandy Ground; turquoise shallows, one beach grill.

🛋️ Shoal Bay West

Quieter; home to the sleek Savi Beach Club.

Live music & nightlife

The weekly rhythm

🎸 Dune Preserve

Bankie Banx's driftwood bar, Rendezvous Bay. Live Fri/Sat + Sun afternoon, Friday BBQ. Closed Mondays.

🎧 Elvis' Beach Bar

Boat-hull bar at Sandy Ground. DJ Kue on Saturdays; Sunday beach BBQ.

🔥 Sunday BBQs

Da'Vida (Crocus Bay) & Elvis' — the classic island Sunday.
Artist line-ups aren't published this far out — the day-of-week pattern is the reliable backbone. Reconfirm a week ahead.
Good to know

Driving & practicalities

🚗 Drive on the left

British territory. Flat, low-traffic, 30 mph limit — most adjust in a day.

🪪 Temp permit

~US$25, arranged by the rental desk. Bring your home license + a card.

🛣️ Roads

Main roads paved; beach tracks sandy/rutted — higher clearance helps, go slow at night.

🗓️ Season

No major festival these dates (Moonsplash = March, Carnival = late July) — relaxed & uncrowded.
